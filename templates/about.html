About WellnessIQ

Revolutionizing health optimization through AI-powered root cause analysis and explainable, actionable lifestyle insights.

Why Traditional Approaches Fail

What started as a research project into lifestyle-induced fatigue quickly evolved into WellnessIQ. We realized that millions of people struggle with low energy, poor sleep, and stress without knowing the root cause.

Generic health trackers just give you raw data. "You slept 5 hours." "You took 4,000 steps." But they don't tell you why you feel tired or what to do about it. We built this platform to connect the dots between your daily habits and your overall health, offering precise, clinical-grade recommendations.

AI-Powered Root Cause Analysis

🔍 Multi-Modal ML Engine

Our platform is powered by a robust Scikit-Learn HistGradientBoostingClassifier trained on thousands of clinical baseline models. It precisely identifies the underlying triggers for fatigue, stress, and poor sleep.

🧠 Generative AI Coach

Once our ML engine identifies the root cause of your lifestyle patterns, our Gemini AI integration steps in to translate raw baseline predictions into highly actionable, easy-to-understand daily protocols.
